Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

The short version

  • This website is a catalog and store information page. It has no accounts, no sign-up or contact forms, no online ordering and no payments.
  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number or address through this website.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies.
  • We do not sell or share personal information from this website, because we don't collect any.

Who we are and what this covers

This policy is from Nature's Nook, a health and nutrition store at 160 E Valley River Rd #2, Rexburg, ID 83440. It covers the website at naturesnook.co, including the coupon page.

It does not cover what happens in the store. If you join our membership program or a mailing list in the store, you give us that information directly. You can ask us at any time what we have on file for you, or ask us to correct or remove it.

Information you give us through the site

None. The search box filters the product list inside your own browser; what you type is not sent to us or saved. The "Send to a friend" button opens your device's own share menu or copies a link, and we never see who you send it to.

Information handled automatically

  • Website hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic browser details when your browser loads a page, and may keep them in its server logs for security and to run the service. We don't receive those logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts. The home page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. To deliver the font files, Google receives your IP address and basic browser details. See the Google Privacy Policy. The policy pages, including this one, do not load Google Fonts.
  • Browser storage. If you close the announcement banner at the top of the home page, the site saves one small note in your browser's local storage so the banner stays closed. It contains no personal information, stays on your device and is never sent to us. See our cookie policy.

Links to other sites

The site links to Google Maps, Apple Maps, our Google reviews and our Facebook page. Nothing is sent to those companies by our site unless you click the link. Once you do, their own privacy policies apply.

Phone calls

If you call the store, we use what you tell us only to help you, for example to check stock or place a special order.

Children

This website is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it.

Your choices

You can clear the saved banner note at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Because the website holds no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to look up, correct or delete on the website side.

Changes to this policy

If the website changes, for example if we add online ordering or a sign-up form, we will update this policy first and change the date at the top.
