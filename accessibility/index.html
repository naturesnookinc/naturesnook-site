Accessibility

Last updated November 12, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard instead of a mouse, larger text or high-contrast settings.

What we aim for

We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, as our target. We reviewed and updated the site in November 2026. We don't claim the site meets every part of the guidelines, and we keep working on it.

What the site does

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, with a visible outline showing where you are and a "Skip to main content" link at the top.
  • Photos have text descriptions, and buttons and links have clear names.
  • Text and background colors are chosen for readable contrast.
  • The photo and review slideshows have a Pause button, and they don't move at all if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • The site works when zoomed in and on small screens.

Known limits

  • Product photos show the package label as a picture. For many products we also give the Supplement Facts as text next to the photo, but not yet for all of them.
  • Links to Google Maps, Apple Maps and Facebook lead to sites we don't control.

If something doesn't work for you

Tell us and we will help you get what you need another way, and fix the problem if we can. We're glad to check stock, read a label or answer product questions by phone.
