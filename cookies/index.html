Cookie policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

The short version

This website does not set any cookies of its own, and it has no analytics or advertising trackers. That is why you don't see a cookie banner.

What the site saves in your browser

One thing. If you close the announcement banner at the top of the home page, the site saves a small note in your browser's local storage (its name starts with nn-month-hidden) so the banner stays closed on your next visit. The note holds no personal information, is not shared with anyone and is not used to track you.

To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser settings. The only effect is that the banner shows again.

Outside services the site loads

  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on the home page. Google receives your IP address and basic browser details to deliver the files. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you click a link to Google Maps, Apple Maps or Facebook, those sites may set their own cookies under their own policies.

Do you need to give consent?

No. We don't use cookies or similar tools for analytics, advertising or tracking, so there is nothing to consent to. If we ever add something that does, we will update this page first and ask for your permission where the law requires it.
