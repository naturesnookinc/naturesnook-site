Gluten-free & natural groceries in Rexburg

Besides vitamins and herbs, Nature's Nook is a natural grocer. Our gluten-free section has breads, baking supplies, snacks and meals, and our shelves carry grains and flours, natural sweeteners, nuts and dried fruit, spices, bulk herbs and healthy snacks. We also fill your jugs with distilled water made in the store.

Always read the package for ingredients and allergens before you buy. If a product you want isn't on the shelf, ask and we'll do our best to order it.
