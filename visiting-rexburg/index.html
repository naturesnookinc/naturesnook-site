Visiting from out of town

Nature's Nook is a local health food store at 160 E Valley River Rd #2 in Rexburg, Idaho. If you are coming from Teton Valley, Jackson or the Idaho Falls area, here is what to know before you make the trip.

Directions

Drive times are approximate and winter roads can add time, so check conditions before you leave.

  • From Driggs (about 50 min)
  • From Victor (about 1 hr)
  • From Jackson (about 1.5 hr)
  • From Idaho Falls (about 30 min)
  • From Rigby (about 20 min)
  • From Ashton (about 35 min)

What is worth the drive

We list thousands of products from hundreds of brands. A few lines we stock in depth:

  • Redmond Re-Lyte electrolyte drink mixes in dozens of flavors and sizes
  • Ancient Nutrition, Terry Naturally, Natural Factors and Trace Minerals
  • Herbs and tinctures, including our own Nature's Nook label
  • Kids and baby products: children's vitamins, kids probiotics, kids toothpaste and baby care
  • Gluten-free foods, baking supplies and natural groceries
  • Essential oils, teas and natural personal care

Before you go

Stock changes day to day. If you are driving a long way for one product, call (208) 356-5284 and ask whether it is on the shelf, or search our online list first. Please read each package for ingredients and allergens before you buy.
